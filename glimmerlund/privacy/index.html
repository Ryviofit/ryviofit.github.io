Privacy Policy

Developer and data controller: ARTICOM IMPORT OG INVEST JENSEN, a Norwegian sole proprietorship owned by Jan Trygve Berg Jensen, organization number 984 762 054. Privacy contact: post@aini.no.

No account, no personal details

Glimmerlund does not require a player account and never asks for your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos or microphone. The game does not run a server of its own; the only services involved are the Google services described below.

Stored on your device

Your level, stars, coins, lives, boosters, daily gift streak, Star Chest progress, settings and purchase status are stored only on the device. Local data is removed when the app is uninstalled or its storage is cleared, and progress cannot be recovered after that.

Game analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To find levels that are too hard and improve the game, the app sends gameplay events to Google Analytics for Firebase: for example levels started, won and failed, stars, boosters and coins used, ads watched and purchases made. Firebase adds an app-instance ID created on the device, the device model, operating system, app version, language and approximate country derived from the IP address. We do not send your name or contact details, and we only look at the data in aggregate. Advertising-related use of this data follows the choice you make in the consent form described below. Event-level data is kept for 2 months; aggregated reports are kept longer. Read Firebase's privacy information.

Reminders (optional)

After a few levels the game asks if you want reminders. If you say yes and allow notifications, the phone itself schedules reminders such as “Your lives are full” and “Your daily gift is ready”. They are created and stored only on the device; no push token or other data is sent anywhere. Turn them off in Settings → Reminders or in the phone's notification settings.

Advertising

The game uses Google AdMob for interstitial ads between levels and ads you choose to watch for rewards (extra lives, coins and more time). Depending on region, your consent choices and device settings, Google and its partners may process device and network information, approximate location derived from an IP address, ad interactions, diagnostics and advertising identifiers to show, measure and secure ads. Where required (EEA, UK and Switzerland), Google's consent form is shown before ads are requested, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy Options. On iPhone, the advertising identifier is only used if you allow tracking. Read Google's Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Apple or Google; we never receive card details or your store account details. The purchase receipt is checked on the device and is not sent to us. Remove Ads and the Starter Pack can be restored with Settings → Restore Purchases.

Legal basis, transfers and retention

Personalised ads are based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. Non-personalised ads and game analytics are based on our legitimate interest in keeping the game free and improving it; you can object by emailing us. Reminders are based on your consent. Google may process data outside the EEA under the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards, and retains data under its own policies and legal obligations. Analytics data is stored by Google on our behalf; we have no other personal data about you.

Your rights and contact

You may ask questions about this notice, object to processing or ask us to help with a request to Google by emailing post@aini.no. You can also complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet).

Children and changes

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed to children. This notice is updated when features, providers or legal requirements change; the date above identifies the current version.
