PLAYER CARE

Support

For gameplay or technical help, email post@aini.no.

Quick answers

  • A purchase is missing: open Settings → Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account. Remove Ads and the Starter Pack come back; coin packs that were already added cannot be restored.
  • Out of lives: lives refill over time. You can also refill them with coins or by watching an ad.
  • Ads and privacy choices: Settings → Privacy Options (shown where your region requires it). On iPhone, tracking can also be changed in the phone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Lost progress: progress is stored on the device only, so it is lost if the app is deleted or its data is cleared.

Before contacting support

  • Update Glimmerlund and your phone's operating system.
  • Include platform, OS version, app version, device model, the level you were on and the approximate time of the issue.

Never send passwords, authentication codes or payment-card details.
