PLAYER CARE

Support

For gameplay or technical help, email post@aini.no.

Quick answers

  • A purchase is missing: open Settings → Restore purchases while signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account.
  • Star Pass: it covers the season it was bought in. After reinstalling, open the game once online and it comes back for that season.
  • No notifications: check Settings → Daily reminders / Friends & league in the game and the app's notification permission in the phone settings.
  • Ads and privacy choices: Settings → Privacy & ad choices (shown where your region requires it).
  • My run wasn't ranked: leaderboards need a connection and the latest version of the game. Offline runs are sent the next time you open the game, while that day's board is open.

Before contacting support

  • Update STARTHREAD and your phone's operating system.
  • Include platform, OS version, app version, device model, the approximate time of the issue and your friend code (Friends tab) if it concerns online features.

Never send passwords, authentication codes or payment-card details.

Delete your online data

Email post@aini.no with your friend code and we will delete your leaderboard, friend and league data. See the Privacy Policy.
