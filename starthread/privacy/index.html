Privacy Policy

Developer and data controller: ARTICOM IMPORT OG INVEST JENSEN, a Norwegian sole proprietorship owned by Jan Trygve Berg Jensen, organization number 984 762 054. Privacy contact: post@aini.no.

No account, no personal details

STARTHREAD does not require a player account and never asks for your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos or microphone. The game does not use developer-operated analytics.

Stored on your device

Progress, shards, skins, settings, streaks, Star Pass progress, notification choices and nicknames you give friends are stored on the device. Friend nicknames never leave the device. Local data is removed when the app is uninstalled or its storage is cleared.

Online features (leaderboards, friends, ghosts, Weekly Cup)

To run the Daily Sky board, friends, ghost races and Weekly Cup leagues, the game sends a server operated by the developer on Cloudflare:

  • a random player ID created on the device (not linked to your identity or device identifiers);
  • a generated display name such as “Lucky Comet 27” (you cannot type a name; you can switch to another generated one);
  • scores and the timing of your taps in a run, used to verify the run by replaying it (anti-cheat) and to show ghosts to friends;
  • your friend code and the friends you add, and your league and group placement.

Daily boards and runs are deleted after about 35 days and Weekly Cup data after 8 weeks. Your friend list and league stay until you remove friends or ask us to delete your data. Other players only see your generated name and scores, never your player ID.

Notifications (optional)

Reminders such as “Daily Sky is up” are scheduled locally on the device. If you allow notifications and keep “Friends & league” on, the device's push token (from Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service), platform, time-zone offset and notification choices are stored on our server, so we can tell you when a friend beats your score, adds you, nudges you or passes you in your league. Turning the option off deletes the token. Unused tokens are deleted after 90 days.

Advertising

The game uses Google AdMob for interstitial ads and ads you choose to watch for rewards. Depending on region, your consent choices and device settings, Google and its partners may process device and network information, approximate location derived from an IP address, ad interactions, diagnostics and advertising identifiers to show, measure and secure ads. Where required, Google's consent form is shown before ads are requested, and you can change your choice in Settings → Privacy & ad choices. On iPhone, the advertising identifier is only used if you allow tracking. Read Google's Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Apple or Google; we never receive card details. To validate purchases and restore them, the game uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and your store purchase history for this app. Read RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Sharing

Sharing a run opens the phone's share sheet only when you choose to. The game never posts anything automatically.

Legal basis, transfers and retention

Online features and anti-cheat are provided under our legitimate interest in operating a fair game you have chosen to play; notifications and personalised ads are based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. Cloudflare, Google and RevenueCat may process data outside the EEA under the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards. Providers retain data under their own policies and legal obligations.

Your rights and contact

You may ask for access to or deletion of the data stored with us, or object to processing. Email post@aini.no and include your friend code (shown on the Friends tab) so we can find your data. You can also complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet).

Children and changes

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed to children. This notice is updated when features, providers or legal requirements change; the date above identifies the current version.
